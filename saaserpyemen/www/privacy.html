1Who we are

{{ brand_name }} operates a hosted ERP platform built on ERPNext. We act as the data controller for account and billing information, and as a data processor for the business data you store inside your workspace. This policy explains our practices across the marketing website and the customer portal.

2What we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email, company name, phone number, country and preferred language.
  • Billing details: plan, billing cycle, currency, payment references and the receipts you upload. We do not store full card numbers because payments are made by bank transfer.
  • Workspace data: the records, files and settings you create inside your ERP, which belong to you.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and timestamps, used for security and diagnostics.
  • Communication: messages you send to support and records of account activity.

3How we use your information

  • To create your account, provision your workspace and keep it running.
  • To process manual payments, verify receipts and manage subscriptions.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To secure the platform, prevent abuse and monitor performance.
  • To send service messages such as trial reminders, payment updates and security notices.
  • To improve the product using aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use the business data in your workspace for advertising.

4Legal basis for processing

We process personal data to perform our contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, to protect legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, and where you have given consent (for example, optional communications). You can withdraw consent at any time.

5Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers who help us run the platform — such as hosting, email delivery and backup providers — under contracts that require them to protect it. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public. We never share one customer's workspace data with another customer.

6Storage and security

Each workspace has its own isolated database and file storage. We encrypt data in transit with HTTPS, restrict administrative access, apply security updates and take daily backups. Passwords are stored using one-way hashing. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and enable any additional protections available to you.

Our servers and backup providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

7Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. We may use a small preference cookie to remember your language and currency choices. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear cookies in your browser at any time, though you will need to sign in again.

8Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and request a portable copy of your data. You can update your company profile from your dashboard. To exercise other rights, email {{ support_email or 'infosahbix@gmail.com' }} and we will respond within the time required by law.

9Retention

We keep account and billing records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. When a workspace is deleted, its database and files are removed after any retention period, and backups are rotated out on our normal backup schedule.

10Children

The service is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or the law. We will post the new version here with an updated date, and where changes are significant we will notify you by email or in your dashboard.

12Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email {{ support_email or 'infosahbix@gmail.com' }}. We take privacy seriously and will do our best to help.
